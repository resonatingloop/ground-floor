# settlement mapping independent analysis amendment

## owner view

- change: separate instrument construction from the study's first analytical
  reading by requiring at least one conversation-independent sub-agent and a
  sealed two-stage reveal.
- before: the accepted base spec permits the primary agent to author, build,
  inspect, and effectively interpret the same fictional specimen. because that
  agent co-developed the method and expected distinction, a successful reading
  could merely replay prior synthesis.
- after: the primary agent may build and steward the instrument but cannot
  supply its independent first reading. a cold analyst records a pre-reveal map
  from a neutral trace, then encounters the authored visualization and returns
  a separate comparison without either record being overwritten.
- authority: the owner accepted the source revision fingerprinted in
  frontmatter and directly authorized implementation. this amendment changes
  only the bounded local visualization study and authorizes only its required
  built-in collaboration sub-agent run with fictional packet material. it does
  not authorize
  private-case analysis, method promotion, external provider api integration,
  publication, commit, push, or deployment.

## why the accepted base needs amendment

the base study asks whether one rendered specimen makes interactional standing,
reverse pressure, non-return, and valence-separating change conditions legible.
the primary agent helped derive those relations and authored the study design.
that participation is useful for construction and provenance but disqualifies
the same session from claiming an impartial or independent first reading.

the defect is not cured by adding a reviewer after the primary has already
interpreted the result. nor is it cured by hiding the visualization from the
reviewer: that would test unaided analysis of a fictional trace rather than the
visual instrument. the smallest honest design is a staged reveal that freezes
an unaided reading before the visualization supplies its authored map.

“cold” is bounded, not metaphysical. it means no inherited conversation, no
participation in method, fixture, or display development, and no access beyond
the declared packet. model training, system instructions, runtime facilities,
workspace-level memory, and unobservable prior exposure remain occluded unless
the runtime exposes them. the study must record that claim ceiling rather than
calling the analyst priorless.

## owner decisions

### 1. separate builder/steward from cold analyst

- recommendation: the primary agent may implement, validate, render, repair,
  and document the instrument but may not serve as its cold analyst or present
  its own reading as independent confirmation.
- alternative: let the primary agent perform the study because the fixture is
  fictional and the authored relations are already explicit.
- consequence: role separation prevents design familiarity from silently
  acquiring the standing of analytical recovery.

the minimum study uses one sub-agent created without inherited conversation.
additional independent analysts may be added later, but one is sufficient for
this bounded legibility study and is not evidence of reliability or generality.

### 2. use a sealed two-stage packet

- recommendation: freeze both stages before the run. stage a contains the
  neutral fictional trace and response protocol; stage b contains the complete
  authored visualization. capture and fingerprint the stage-a return before
  revealing stage b to the same analyst.
- alternative: provide the full visualization immediately or ask a different
  analyst to compare it with an unseen baseline.
- consequence: one reader supplies a source-local baseline and a post-reveal
  account while stable identifiers preserve correspondence across stages.

### 3. preserve disagreement through concordance

- recommendation: store the stage-a return, stage-b return, and comparison
  ledger as distinct immutable records. the primary agent may create a
  concordance but may not revise the analyst's language into the reference map.
- alternative: conduct follow-up dialogue until the analyst and builder agree.
- consequence: omission, alternative reading, packet ambiguity, and
  display-induced cueing remain study results rather than defects to be
  conversationally repaired away.

## roles and claim ceilings

| role | may do | may not claim or do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owner | accept or reject specs, approve later claims, judge usefulness, leave disagreement unresolved | delegate acceptance authority to review marks or generated artifacts |
| primary builder/steward | implement, validate, render, freeze packets, record hashes, spawn the cold analyst, preserve raw returns, and author a labeled concordance | act as cold analyst, call its own reading independent, coach the analyst between stages, or overwrite raw returns |
| cold analyst | inspect only the staged packet, return source-local accounts, name alternatives and insufficiencies, and compare after reveal | inspect repository rationale, private sources, prior reviews, expected answers, or decide method validity |
| verifier | check packet identity, exposure declarations, hashes, ordering, and unchanged returns | adjudicate the analytical disagreement merely because protocol checks pass |

owner adjudication is not required for every divergence. the owner may accept
the instrument as useful while preserving an unresolved alternative reading.

## frozen packet and exposure manifest

both stages are prepared before the analyst is spawned and listed in one
manifest with sha-256 fingerprints.

stage a may contain only:

- a neutral wholly fictional trace with stable scenario and moment identifiers;
- declared slice, projection, attribution policy, and occlusion;
- short operational definitions needed to parse the task;
- the seven study questions from the base spec; and
- a fixed response form requiring support, alternative, claim ceiling,
  non-claim, and explicit insufficiency.

stage a excludes:

- the builder's standing objects, dependency edges, return-path stages,
  protective/stale labels, interpretation, and answer key;
- the base spec, this amendment, conceptual checkpoint, design discussion,
  implementation history, prior reviews, and owner reactions; and
- private sources, private wording, local archive paths, and unrelated files.

stage b contains the complete built visualization and a neutral request to
inspect what it adds, changes, obscures, or overdetermines. it may not include
feedback on the stage-a response, a desired correction, or a builder-authored
summary of agreement.

the analyst receives a disposable packet path, not repository navigation
instructions. its exposure declaration names every supplied file, whether any
other file was opened, the absence of inherited conversation, and the known
occlusions around system, training, runtime, and workspace memory.

## study run lifecycle

1. validate the fictional fixture and build both packet stages.
2. freeze the packet manifest and hashes before spawning an analyst.
3. spawn at least one sub-agent without inherited conversation and provide only
   stage a plus the exposure instructions.
4. receive and preserve the analyst's complete stage-a return before any
   follow-up, explanation, or stage-b path is sent.
5. continue with the same analyst, reveal only stage b, and request the separate
   post-reveal account and self-comparison.
6. preserve the complete stage-b return without repair dialogue.
7. create a labeled concordance that classifies each relation as agreement,
   omission, alternative reading, packet ambiguity, display-induced cueing, or
   unresolved.
8. verify identities, ordering, hashes, exposure declaration, and unchanged
   raw returns before presenting the run to the owner.

if the analyst reports undeclared exposure, opens a forbidden source, inherits
the conversation, sees stage b before the stage-a return is preserved, or is
coached between stages, the run is contaminated. retain it as protocol evidence
but do not count it as the required cold run.

## outputs and evidence status

the stage-a return records:

- candidate standing object or relation;
- downstream reliance and supporting moment identifiers;
- reverse pressure and the relation it touches;
- persistence, change, translation, release, or insufficiency;
- governing change condition and a bounded valence reading when supportable;
- alternatives, uncertainty, claim ceiling, and explicit non-claims; and
- ambiguity or usability friction in the neutral packet.

the stage-b return records what the visualization made newly available, what it
made easier, what it biased or overdetermined, remaining disagreement, and
display or interaction friction. the comparison ledger links both returns to
the builder-authored reference without replacing any of them.

these records may evidence what one bounded, declared-exposure analyst could
recover from this fictional specimen and what changed after the visualization
reveal. they do not validate settlement mapping as a method, prove occurrence
in private cases, establish causal mechanism, reliability, prevalence,
generality, or model interior state.

## scope

- require at least one no-inherited-conversation sub-agent run;
- preserve pre-reveal, post-reveal, exposure, and concordance records
  separately in ignored study output;

- claiming absolute impartiality, blank priors, or hidden-context control;

## implementation by invariant

### 1. the builder cannot become the cold analyst by changing labels

- record builder/steward and analyst identifiers separately;
- require the analyst to be spawned without inherited conversation; and
- refuse a run when the same session fills both roles.

**proof:** protocol tests cover distinct roles and reject self-analysis.

### 2. stage b cannot ratchet backward into stage a

- build and hash both stages before the run;
- preserve the complete stage-a return before disclosing stage b; and
- never edit the stage-a record after reveal.

**proof:** ordering and fingerprint tests reject premature reveal, missing
returns, altered packets, and rewritten records.

### 3. exposure is declared rather than idealized

- give the analyst an exact allowlist and disposable packet path;
- collect a file-level exposure declaration; and
- record hidden system, training, runtime, and workspace context as occluded.

**proof:** the run receipt distinguishes verified packet exposure from
unverifiable prior context and marks undeclared access contaminated.

### 4. concordance cannot manufacture consensus

- retain raw stage-a and stage-b returns unchanged;
- classify divergence without deleting the analyst's alternative; and
- reserve usefulness and method claims to the owner.

**proof:** every concordance entry links both source records, permits
unresolved status, and contains no write path back into them.

## done when

   required cold-analyst role.
2. a deterministic manifest fingerprints both frozen stages before the run.
3. stage a contains only the allowlisted neutral material and stage b contains
   the complete visualization without response-specific coaching.
4. at least one sub-agent with no inherited conversation completes stage a
   before receiving stage b.

   distinct and fingerprinted.

## risks and unknowns

- the fictional trace and neutral definitions are still designed objects and
  may cue the intended distinction before visualization reveal.
- a no-inherited-conversation sub-agent may still carry workspace memories or
  general priors unavailable to the run steward.
- the built-in collaboration runtime makes the fictional packet and task
  visible to its model service even though no application-level provider api
  integration is introduced.
- one analyst can reveal legibility failures and alternatives but cannot
  estimate inter-analyst reliability.
- the stage-a response form may itself teach the ontology it is meant to test.
- a post-reveal self-comparison may rationalize rather than faithfully report
  what changed.
- strict packet isolation is procedural, not a filesystem security boundary.
- the owner's own theoretical saturation remains relevant when interpreting
  concordance and deciding usefulness.

## open questions

- none block owner review. whether one cold analyst is sufficient for this
  sprint is resolved by the owner's minimum of at least one; adding more later
  is a separate scope choice.
