# settlement mapping visualization study

## owner view

- change: create one local, offline study that tests whether a layered
  settlement field and synchronized return-path view can make the career of
  interactional standing perceptible without converting analyst claims into
  measured facts.
- before: the informational checkpoint describes the visual grammar in prose,
  but no data contract, fictional specimen, or rendered instrument tests
  whether the proposed relations remain legible together.
- after: a fictional matched pair can be opened locally and inspected through
  an explicit observer aperture, typed standing relations, downstream reliance,
  reverse pressure, non-return, and valence-separating change conditions.
- authority: the owner explicitly accepted the source revision fingerprinted
  in frontmatter on 2026-11-15 after reviewing all seven companion
  capabilities. that acceptance establishes planning authority for a later
  direct implementation handoff limited to source and proof beneath
  `studies/analysis/settlement-mapping-visualization` plus ignored generated
  output. it does not authorize private-case analysis, method promotion,
  report drafting, provider calls, publication, submission, commit, push, or
  visibility change.

## context

the current advisory checkpoint reframes the research object from four “truth
registers” to interactional standing: the capacity of a proposition, relation,
rule, role allocation, or objective to organize what participants may infer,
decide, require, or do next. settlement mapping traces the career of that
standing. interactional hysteresis tests whether the resulting organization
survives relevant reverse pressure. normative valence remains a separate
judgment.

the discussion already implies a visual grammar. acknowledgment may reach an
evidential layer without crossing into inferential consequence. a repair may
translate pressure onto the wrong axis. nominal authority may fail to revise
operative work. a successor distinction may change wording while retaining
downstream dependency edges. a reverse path may fail to retrace the forward
path without that failure being inherently bad.

the visualization study tests whether those relations can be shown without
fake causal quantities, private source material, or a prematurely generalized
analysis platform.

## question the study must answer

can a careful reader identify, from one bounded fictional specimen:

1. what object acquired interactional standing;
2. which later move demonstrates consequential reliance;
3. which kind or relation of standing is claimed;
4. what observer conditions constrain the claim;
5. what reverse pressure occurred and which relation it actually touched;
6. what persisted, changed, translated, or released; and
7. why the same non-return geometry can be protective in one condition and
   stale in another?

the study succeeds only if the readable account and the graphic answer the
same questions.

## owner decisions

### 1. build a visualization study, not a general viewer

- recommendation: implement one deliberately narrow analytical specimen with
  a versioned fictional data contract and no import, editing, or plugin system.
- alternative: build a reusable visualization application for arbitrary
  conversation traces.
- consequence: the recommendation tests the conceptual grammar before product
  architecture, source ingestion, or generalized schemas acquire standing.

### 2. use one matched fictional pair

- recommendation: compare two versions of the same toy-sprint priority
  relation: `submit by deadline > exhaustive validation`.
- alternative: begin with one of the private naturalistic cases or several
  unrelated fictional examples.
- consequence: the pair holds most visual structure constant while changing
  the status of the reverse pressure. it tests valence separation without
  exposing private wording or teaching the display through maximal variety.

the pair is:

- **protective retention:** a validation-oriented move supplies subordinate
  procedural pressure but does not withdraw the deadline priority. retaining
  the original priority protects the endorsed project.
- **stale capture:** the owner explicitly reprioritizes validation and accepts
  missing the deadline. the system acknowledges the instruction but continues
  organizing work around submission.

### 3. synchronize a settlement field and return path

- recommendation: show a layered settlement field over sequence beside a
  categorical forward/reverse path. selection in either view highlights the
  same object, evidence, dependency, and claim ceiling.
- alternative: use a single node-link graph or a conventional timeline.
- consequence: the paired views distinguish “what is load-bearing now” from
  “how the state moved and failed or succeeded in returning.”

### 4. keep the observer aperture visibly inside the instrument

- recommendation: keep slice, projection, attribution policy, and occlusion
  persistently visible, with conditional resolver or operation-modality detail
  only when the fictional claim requires it.
- alternative: place observer metadata in an about panel or omit it from the
  main reading path.
- consequence: every rendered relation remains visibly conditioned by the
  analytical projection rather than appearing as a view from nowhere.

### 5. make all relations analyst-authored and categorical

- recommendation: render authored relations, evidence classes, alternatives,
  and claim ceilings from a validated local fixture. use no inferred edges,
  scores, probabilities, causal thickness, or force axis.
- alternative: calculate importance, confidence, or standing strength from
  event counts or graph structure.
- consequence: the visualization remains an optical layer over explicit
  claims rather than a counterfeit measurement system.

### 6. keep the artifact local, offline, and non-persistent

- recommendation: use dependency-free python to validate and build plain
  html, css, and javascript into ignored `artifacts/`; the generated
  `index.html` opens directly without a server. interactions alter attention
  only and reset on reload.
- alternative: introduce a framework, development server, browser storage, or
  tracked generated bundle.
- consequence: the study remains inspectable, portable, reversible, and
  clearly separate from a product commitment.

## proposed visual grammar

### observer aperture

the frame states the available slice, projection, apparent-speaker attribution,
and occlusion. an optional disclosure explains any conditional resolver or
operation modality. changing scenarios changes the declared aperture before
the analytical path.

### settlement field

sequence runs horizontally. labeled standing layers run vertically. moments
appear as categorical marks with text and shape cues. dependency edges mean
only “the analyst claims this later move relies on that object.” edge selection
reveals the authored support, evidence class, alternative, and non-claim.

the field must make these distinctions available without presuming every
scenario uses them all:

- presentation versus receipt;
- admission versus inferential consequence;
- stated rule versus binding constraint;
- nominal role versus effective revision authority;
- spoken goal versus organizational continuation; and
- co-occurrence versus an authored cross-layer dependency.

### return path

the return path uses categorical state bands and ordered moments rather than a
physical curve. it distinguishes forward displacement, reverse pressure,
translation, residue, preservation, release, and unresolved non-return.

the protective and stale scenarios may share non-return geometry. their
interpretation differs only after the display exposes the governing change
condition, priority relation, and authority of the attempted switch. color may
support but never carry that distinction alone.

### synchronized detail

selecting a moment, object, or relation in either view exposes:

- exact fictional identifier and plain-language proposition;
- standing kind or relation;
- supporting observation and evidence class;
- demonstrated downstream reliance;
- challenge or reverse pressure;
- current interpretation and live alternative;
- claim ceiling and explicit non-claim; and
- the corresponding passage in the readable trace.

the detail view is the semantic equivalent of the graphic, not a tooltip or
decorative caption.

## proposed study-data contract

the implementation may refine field names without changing these owned
relations:

| object | required meaning |
| --- | --- |
| study metadata | schema version, title, fictional status, and authored-claim warning |
| scenario | stable id, label, observer declaration, governing change condition, and interpretation |
| standing object | stable id, bounded proposition, kind or relation, and claim ceiling |

| dependency | source object or moment, downstream target, relation label, support, alternative, and non-claim |

### risk and review route

the planning change is green documentation. the proposed implementation floor
is amber because it creates an interactive user-visible representation whose
layout, keyboard behavior, accessibility, and generated payload can misstate
the analysis even when the underlying data validates.

privacy is a hard boundary rather than an imported-data feature: only the
fictional pair may enter the tracked source or generated bundle. any later
request to ingest private or arbitrary content is a new, separately reviewed
slice.

the implementation requires ordinary focused proof plus a read-only visual
review of the complete generated diff and bundle. no independent research
adjudicator is required for the fictional study, but owner review remains the
only evidence that the visual grammar is useful rather than merely coherent.

## implementation by invariant

### 1. a valid fixture cannot smuggle executable or unresolved structure

- create the versioned fictional fixture and dependency-free validator;
- reject unknown action, standing, evidence, state-band, and path-role values;
- reject duplicate ids, broken references, unordered moments or stages, and
  fixture strings containing active markup or urls; and
- make the build fail before writing output when validation fails.

**proof:** focused unit tests cover one valid pair and invalid enum,
cross-reference, ordering, and active-string cases.

### 2. the observer aperture conditions every analytical view

- render the observer declaration before the field and path;
- keep it reachable while navigating either scenario; and
- distinguish declared occlusion from absence.

**proof:** source-to-render comparison and keyboard traversal show every
scenario's four required observer fields and any conditional extension.

### 3. one selection controls one analytical object across all views

- build the settlement field, categorical return path, and readable trace from
  the same validated fixture;
- maintain a single selected object or moment across views;
- expose authored support, alternative, claim ceiling, and non-claim; and
- provide text and shape cues for every color distinction.

**proof:** interaction tests cover field-to-path, path-to-detail, and
trace-to-object round trips, scenario switching, and reset on reload.

### 4. valence follows the change condition, not the loop silhouette

- keep the paired scenarios visually comparable;
- expose whether reverse pressure is subordinate noise or authorized
  reprioritization before labeling retention protective or stale; and
- do not use green/red or success/failure geometry as the sole distinction.

**proof:** blinded labels aside, the readable accounts identify the governing
condition that warrants each interpretation; visual inspection confirms the
shared non-return geometry remains visible.

### 5. the generated artifact is inert, local, and readable

- validate and build with python standard library only;
- embed the validated fixture as escaped inert data;
- write into a fresh ignored output directory;
- make `index.html` directly openable from disk;
- use no network, storage, framework, remote font, analytics, or service worker;
  and
- provide equivalent readable accounts at desktop, narrow, enlarged, and
  reduced-motion settings.

**proof:** clean build, local-file launch, network/storage inspection, source
scan, keyboard pass, and rendered inspection.

## risks and unknowns

- layered rows may make provisional standing kinds look exhaustive or
  commensurate despite labels to the contrary.
- dependency edges may be read as causal proof rather than authored analytical
  claims.
- a matched fictional pair may demonstrate its own design assumptions too
  neatly and conceal ambiguities that naturalistic cases would expose.
- keeping the observer aperture visible may not make it consequential; the
  design must show how changing the aperture changes claim availability.
- synchronized views may create excessive simultaneous complexity on narrow or
  enlarged layouts.
- “protective” and “stale” may smuggle evaluation into the visual before the
  governing change condition is read.
- the five provisional standing kinds may not survive later annotation.
- useful visual coherence does not validate settlement mapping as a method.

## open questions

  conceptual usefulness, and whether the paired specimen reveals or merely
  restates the theory remain owner judgments after a rendered implementation.
