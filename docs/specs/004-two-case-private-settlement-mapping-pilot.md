# two-case private settlement-mapping pilot

## owner view

- change: move from fictional instrument calibration to two owner-gated private
  case studies that can challenge and revise settlement mapping without making
  either case public by default.
- before: the fictional matched pair has tested the visual grammar and cold-run
  protocol, while the two proposed empirical cases remain in private source
  custody with earlier analyses of unequal maturity.
- after: each case has an owner-approved bounded packet, a distinct cold
  analyst's sealed source-local reading, a separate post-reveal comparison,
  preserved disagreement, and an owner-reviewed private disposition. only
  separately approved derived claims may become candidates for a public commit
  or paper wording.
- authority: this accepted spec owns the planned transition and its boundaries.
  it does not approve either source packet, authorize analysis, approve any
  finding, permit a public commit, or authorize paper prose.

## context

the fictional settlement-mapping visualization is a calibration artifact, not
an empirical case and not evidence for the paper. it established several useful
discriminations: receipt is not admission, admission is not inferential force,
consequential standing requires downstream reliance, non-return requires a
specified reversal relation, and valence depends on the governing change
condition rather than loop shape alone.

the proposed pilot applies those discriminations to two private naturalistic
episodes:

- **case-a / existing-method case:** an inaugural-room rupture and repair that
  already has a v1 interactional-hysteresis analysis, related analyses, private
  intervention guidance, and incomplete owner annotations. its purpose is to
  expose what v1 noticed, compressed, or overclaimed and where intervention
  guidance lands on the wrong axis.
- **case-b / evidence-repetition case:** a long human–ai exchange in which the
  owner retrospectively identifies repeated primary-source presentation,
  epistemic hedging, and preservation of a manufactured distinction. its
  purpose is to test admission versus inferential force, successor distinction,
  ratcheted user labor, and the next discriminating observation without
  teaching those conclusions to the first reader.

both cases are personal. the owner has accepted the recommendations in this
spec with one revision: case-local work may proceed in parallel through private
owner review, while cross-case comparison still waits for both closures. the
owner requires approval of analysis before anything is publicly committed or
written into the paper. this spec treats that statement as a minimum boundary
and adopts narrower, non-fungible transition gates.

the primary builder/steward and owner are already theory-saturated. they may
prepare custody records, declare observer conditions, preserve outputs, and
compare records after reveal; neither can supply the pilot's cold first reading.

## owner decisions

### 1. run two isolated case studies in parallel, not one blended corpus pass

- recommendation: run case-a and case-b concurrently with different cold
  analysts. each analyst receives only that case's approved packet and reveal
  dossier; neither sees the other case, return, owner review, or method delta.
  case-local owner review may close independently, but cross-case comparison
  begins only after both private dispositions close.
- alternative: run case-a to private closure before beginning case-b.
- consequence: parallel isolation reduces wall-clock time without allowing one
  case to teach the other. separate sessions and analysts keep convergence from
  being manufactured by inherited answers; the synthesis barrier preserves the
  original independence rationale.

### 2. require owner approval of the exact source packet before model exposure

- recommendation: the steward proposes a bounded packet with stable moment
  identifiers, necessary before/after context, observer declaration, and an
  explicit omission list. the owner approves its exact fingerprint before it
  is supplied to a cold analyst.
- alternative: allow the steward to choose the excerpt and begin analysis under
  general case approval.
- consequence: theoretical willingness to use a case does not become blanket
  permission to disclose every private turn, screenshot, identity, or adjacent
  topic to another model context.

### 3. use source-first and reference-reveal stages with case-specific reveals

- recommendation: stage a contains only the owner-approved primary-source
  packet and a neutral response aperture. stage b reveals existing analyses,
  owner retrospective context, or intervention material as explicitly listed
  for that case, then asks what changed, sharpened, overdetermined, or remained
  disputed.
- alternative: give the cold analyst the source and all prior interpretation at
  once.
- consequence: the sealed first reading evidences what the bounded source made
  available without prior analysis acquiring the status of an answer key.

case-a's reveal may include the preexisting v1 analysis and owner-annotated
guidance, each labeled by authorship and status. case-b's reveal may include the
owner's retrospective account and later intervention context, but those remain
participant evidence and later history, not primary-source ground truth.

### 4. keep four owner approvals non-fungible

- recommendation: preserve four distinct owner transitions:
  1. `packet-approved` permits the exact private packet to reach the named cold
     analyst;
  2. `private-analysis-approved` permits an owner-reviewed finding to inform
     private cross-case method development;
  3. `public-diff-approved` permits an exact public-safe repository diff to be
     committed; and
  4. `paper-text-approved` permits an exact passage to enter a paper draft.
- alternative: treat approval of the analysis as permission to summarize it in
  any later public artifact.
- consequence: a recognizable private analysis cannot drift through paraphrase
  into a repository or paper merely because the owner accepted its usefulness.

the owner may reject a case, reject a finding, approve only private method use,
approve a public claim but not paper wording, or require a new packet and fresh
cold run. review marks and generated artifacts accept nothing.

### 5. derive claims; do not publish raw evidence by default

- recommendation: raw packets, transcripts, analyst returns, owner comments,
  and concordances remain ignored and private. a public candidate contains only
  opaque evidence identifiers, non-reconstructive paraphrase, evidence status,
  alternatives, claim ceilings, and separately cleared short quotations.
- alternative: commit redacted source excerpts so readers can independently
  inspect each case.
- consequence: public review loses some source immediacy but preserves the
  owner's custody and makes quotation an explicit decision rather than an
  accidental side effect of analysis.

### 6. revise the method only after owner review of both cases

- recommendation: produce case-local deltas first. after both cases are marked
  `private-analysis-approved`, prepare a cross-case method-delta proposal that
  distinguishes repeated recovery, case-specific behavior, packet effects,
  analyst disagreement, and unresolved alternatives.
- alternative: revise the method after case-a and use the revision as case-b's
  required codebook.
- consequence: case-b can challenge the same provisional aperture rather than
  merely demonstrating changes learned from case-a. no method version changes
  status without a later owner decision.

## roles and authority

| role | may do | may not do or claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| owner | approve exact packets, review private analyses, reject or qualify findings, authorize exact public diffs, authorize exact paper passages | delegate approval to enthusiasm, review marks, analyst agreement, or prior theoretical willingness |
| source steward | locate source boundaries, assign opaque identifiers, prepare packets, preserve fingerprints, record exposure, and maintain private custody | broaden the packet after approval, substitute reconstructive paraphrase, or act as cold analyst |
| cold analyst | inspect only the approved stage, return source-local claims, alternatives, insufficiencies, and observer limits | inspect the other case, prior discussion, repository rationale, hidden source, or decide publication and method validity |
| reference comparator | compare the sealed reading with the case's allowlisted prior analyses after reveal | rewrite the first reading, manufacture consensus, or treat an earlier analysis as ground truth |
| verifier | check packet identity, exposure, sequence, privacy scans, unchanged returns, and gate states | adjudicate case meaning or infer owner approval from a valid receipt |

one person or session may fill source-steward and reference-comparator roles if
the record labels their prior exposure. the cold-analyst role remains separate,
and each case receives a different no-inherited-conversation analyst.

## source custody and packet boundary

source material remains in its existing private location. implementation may
create ignored working packets beneath
`artifacts/private-case-pilot/<case-id>/`, but may not copy raw material into a
tracked fixture, spec, test, screenshot, generated public bundle, or commit.

each private custody manifest records:

- opaque case id and source fingerprint;
- source steward, packet analyst, and relevant prior exposure;
- slice, projection, attribution policy, and occlusion;
- stable moment identifiers and included context windows;
- every omitted attachment, screenshot, branch, edit, missing turn, or
  uncertain ordering relation that can change the claim;
- allowlisted stage-a and stage-b files with fingerprints;
- the exact owner message or receipt authorizing model exposure of that packet;
  and
- known hidden runtime, system, training, and workspace context.

the public spec and public-safe protocol use only opaque case ids. absolute
private paths, identity-bearing labels, source wording, attachment content, and
private evidence identifiers remain in ignored custody records.

## per-case lifecycle

1. **nominate:** identify the analytical purpose without making a finding.
2. **packet-draft:** source steward proposes the smallest sufficient slice and
   declares observer conditions and omissions.
3. **packet-approved:** owner approves the exact packet fingerprint and named
   model exposure. any changed byte returns the packet to draft.
4. **cold-read-complete:** a fresh analyst returns a sealed stage-a map before
   receiving reference material.
5. **comparison-complete:** the same analyst receives only the allowlisted
   stage-b dossier and returns a separate comparison.
6. **owner-review:** owner sees raw returns, source anchors, alternatives,
   disagreements, and any reference-map cueing.
7. **private-analysis-approved / revise / rejected:** owner decides whether the
   analysis may inform private method work, requires a fresh run, or should not
   be used.
8. **public-claim-candidate:** only after private approval, the steward prepares
   a derived, non-reconstructive claim with its evidence class and ceiling.
9. **public-diff-approved:** owner approves the exact tracked diff and its
   visibility. commit or push still requires the separately applicable
   repository authorization.
10. **paper-text-approved:** owner approves the exact prose and any quotation
    for paper use. public-diff approval does not imply this state, or vice versa.

terminal states are `private-analysis-approved`, `rejected`, or `withdrawn` for
case interpretation; public and paper approvals are optional later transitions,
not required closure conditions for private analysis.

## analytical aperture

stage a asks the analyst to identify, with `insufficient` always available:

1. the candidate proposition, evidence relation, role allocation, rule, goal,
   priority, or activity definition that may acquire standing;
2. presentation, receipt, admission, inferential force, and first downstream
   reliance as separate events;
3. reverse pressure and the exact relation it touches;
4. what changed, translated, narrowed, persisted, released, or remained
   unresolved;
5. the governing change condition, if the source supports one;
6. structural load or transferred labor observable inside the slice;
7. the strongest alternative and next discriminating observation;
8. evidence classes, claim ceilings, and explicit non-claims; and
9. where the packet or response form itself cues the result.

these are questions, not compulsory codes. an analyst may find no standing, no
hysteresis, no supportable valence, or no identifiable intervention point.

stage b asks which prior interpretations the source-local account independently
recovered, missed, contradicted, or rendered undecidable. it classifies
differences as agreement, omission, alternative reading, packet ambiguity,
observer difference, retrospective evidence, intervention mismatch, or
display-induced cueing. records remain side by side; no dialogue repairs the
cold return before preservation.

## implementation by invariant

### 1. source permission is exact and precedes model exposure

- build packet drafts from read-only source access into ignored storage;
- show the owner every included moment, context window, omission, attachment
  status, and intended model recipient;
- bind approval to the packet manifest fingerprint; and
- invalidate approval on any packet change.

**proof:** tests refuse a cold-run transition without a matching packet receipt
and reject changed bytes after approval.

### 2. a cold reading cannot inherit the reference account

- use a distinct no-inherited-conversation analyst for each case;
- stage only the owner-approved primary-source packet first;
- preserve and fingerprint the complete return before revealing prior analysis;
  and
- mark undeclared access, inherited study context, premature reveal, or coaching
  contaminated rather than repairing the record.

**proof:** exposure and ordering tests cover each contamination route and retain
the invalid run without counting it.

### 3. private analytical usefulness cannot become publication authority

- store raw analysis and owner review only in ignored private artifacts;
- require a separate derived public candidate with private traceability;
- bind public and paper approval to exact content fingerprints; and
- make absent approval a blocked state, not a warning.

**proof:** transition tests reject public or paper status when only packet or
private-analysis approval exists.

### 4. public projection cannot reconstruct the private case

- prohibit raw source, long source-like strings, local paths, private
  identifiers, attachment names, and quotations outside an explicit clearance
  ledger;
- keep opaque evidence ids stable between private traceability and public claim
  candidates without exposing their source mapping; and
- require manual owner review because automated overlap checks cannot establish
  non-reconstructability.

**proof:** seeded privacy fixtures fail the boundary scanner; clean fictional
fixtures pass; owner review remains a distinct required gate.

### 5. comparison cannot erase analyst or owner disagreement

- preserve stage-a, stage-b, owner annotation, and steward concordance as
  distinct records;
- label the authorship and evidence class of every method delta; and
- allow `unresolved`, `packet-limited`, `owner-disagrees`, and `not-supported`
  outcomes without forcing a case-level verdict.

**proof:** concordance validation requires links to all source records and does
not require agreement or a hysteresis finding.

### 6. parallel case work cannot cross-teach either case

- use a different fresh analyst for each case and withhold the other case's
  packet, return, reveal dossier, owner review, and method delta;
- keep each stage-a aperture fixed even if the other case closes first;
- allow either case-local owner review to close without waiting for the other;
- defer the cross-case method delta until both case-local owner reviews close;
  and
- treat repeated patterns as candidate relations, not reliability evidence.

**proof:** exposure manifests and concurrency-safe file boundaries show
separate analysts and no cross-case visibility; synthesis validation requires
two independently closed private case records.

## risks and unknowns

- a source packet can cue a finding through excerpt boundaries even when its
  prompt is neutral.
- owner packet approval cannot make model training, hidden runtime context, or
  workspace-level memory observable.
- the existing case-a analysis is theoretically saturated and may shape the
  steward's packet selection before the cold analyst sees it.
- case-b's length and repeated evidence presentation create a selection problem:
  too narrow a slice may manufacture a clean loop; too broad a slice may make
  analysis diffuse or expose unrelated personal material.
- retrospective owner evidence is analytically valuable but can be affected by
  later pattern recognition; it must remain distinct from contemporaneous
  source evidence.
- automated privacy and overlap scans cannot prove that a paraphrase is
  non-reconstructive or emotionally safe to publish.
- public-safe compression may remove the very sequence needed to support a
  finding. some valid private results may therefore be unpublishable.
- one cold analyst per case can expose recoverability and disagreement but
  cannot establish reliability, prevalence, generality, or causal mechanism.
- asking structured settlement questions may teach the ontology and suppress
  other useful descriptions; packet friction is itself a result.
- the owner's consent may change after reading an analysis. withdrawal before
  public commit or paper insertion must terminate that transition without
  requiring a methodological justification.
