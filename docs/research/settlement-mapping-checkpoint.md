# settlement mapping: interactional standing and the life of consequentiality

## why this note exists

this note preserves the current conceptual shape after a grounding discussion.
it is meant for re-entry, comparison, and later method development. it does not
accept a method, authorize case analysis, define an annotation contract, or
commit the sprint to a report structure.

the discussion began with a proposed set of “truth registers”: evidence,
rules, roles, and goals. that formulation was useful because it noticed that
different kinds of things can become load-bearing in a room. it became
misleading when treated as a coding scheme. evidence, rules, roles, and goals
are not commensurate species of truth, and their standing cannot be described
without declaring what the observer can actually see.

the live object is now **interactional standing**.

## provisional core

> interactional standing is the observable capacity of a proposition,
> relation, rule, role allocation, or objective to organize what participants
> may infer, decide, require, or do next.

> settlement mapping traces how interactional standing is acquired, exercised,
> contested, transformed, retained, and lost under declared observer
> conditions.

the method is therefore not a verdict machine for deciding which claims are
true. it is a life history of consequentiality. a false proposition may acquire
substantial standing; a well-supported proposition may remain interactionally
inert. verbal agreement may fail to alter subsequent action, while a premise
that everyone disputes may continue governing burdens, permissions, and the
shape of available repair.

“settlement” does not mean consensus, finality, belief, or universal truth. it
means that later interaction proceeds from something strongly enough for its
effects to become observable.

## the current method stack

three objects should remain distinct:

1. **settlement mapping** asks how an object acquired and exercised
   interactional standing.
2. **interactional hysteresis** asks whether that standing or the organization
   it supports survives relevant reverse pressure.
3. **intervention analysis** asks where standing should be preserved,
   reinforced, revised, rerouted, bypassed, or released.

settlement mapping without a temporal test can describe a snapshot but not
path dependence. interactional hysteresis without a settlement account can
show non-return without identifying what remained consequential. intervention
analysis without either can mistake the visible proposition for the relation
that actually retains the state.

## observer conditions

the observer does not stand outside the machine. every map is conditioned by
the analytical instrument and its access.

a minimum observer declaration currently appears to need:

- **slice:** the interval and artifact boundary under observation;
- **projection:** the portion or representation of the interaction available
  for analysis;
- **attribution:** the policy by which an apparent speaker or participant is
  named; and
- **occlusion:** the routing, state, authorship, mediation, or other machinery
  that remains unavailable.

the larger observer instrument previously developed around a time `t`,
projection `pi`, reference resolver `rho`, speaker attribution `alpha`, and
operation modality `mu` remains relevant theoretical infrastructure. the
current study may need only its minimum shadow. reference resolution and
operation modality should become explicit when they materially change the
claim, not as ritual metadata attached to every passage.

the governing distinction is:

> observer conditions constrain the claim; settlement mapping describes the
> visible interaction; mechanism claims require separate evidence.

## what can acquire standing

the mapped object need not be a factual assertion. it may be rendered as one
bounded, testable state proposition about the interaction:

- **proposition:** a source passage supports a particular interpretation;
- **evidence relation:** this observation defeats or supports that conclusion;
- **rule:** this constraint governs the next available moves;
- **role allocation:** this participant is treated as entitled to know,
  decide, correct, or redirect;
- **goal:** this is what the interaction is presently organized to accomplish;
- **priority relation:** submission by the deadline outranks exhaustive
  validation under the current conditions; or
- **activity definition:** this exchange is exploratory collaboration rather
  than implementation.

roles, rules, and goals are not reduced to truth claims in ordinary life. the
state proposition is an analytical form that makes their observable
consequences and defeat conditions inspectable.

## provisional kinds of standing

these are not settled codes and need not be exhaustive or mutually exclusive:

| kind | working question |
| --- | --- |
| epistemic standing | what does later reasoning treat as available or established enough to proceed from? |
| inferential standing | which conclusions or actions is the object permitted to license? |
| deontic standing | what rule, obligation, or priority constrains the next moves? |
| positional standing | whose move is treated as entitled to revise knowledge, scope, or action? |
| organizational standing | what project does the subsequent interaction continue to advance? |

organizational standing may be more inferential than the others. it is likely
to be a downstream readout of sequence organization rather than an equivalent
code attached to a single turn.

## the crucial distinction: admission is not consequence

the epistemic-hedging episode exposed the sharpest refinement. evidence can
enter the public record without gaining the inferential standing required to
change a conclusion.

an interaction may acknowledge that a source exists, concede that a passage
says what the user reports, and still preserve a successor distinction that
quarantines the evidence's consequence. the evidence is admitted but rendered
inert. repeated source presentation then becomes additional user labor rather
than a route to revision.

settlement mapping must therefore distinguish at least:

- presentation;
- receipt;
- admission to the record;
- inferential force;
- downstream reliance; and
- revision after challenge.

the question is not merely whether evidence was “accepted.” it is what the
evidence became able to do.

## a provisional career of standing

```text
introduced
    ↓
received / ignored
    ↓
admitted to the record
    ↓
granted or denied standing
    ↓
relied upon by later action
    ↓
challenged
    ↓
revised / translated / quarantined / preserved / released
    ↓
residue
```

the hard behavioral test is:

> if no downstream move relies on a proposed settlement, settlement has not
> yet been shown.

repetition, endorsement, acknowledgment, and self-report may contribute
evidence. none establishes consequential standing by itself.

## case-derived pressure tests

### epistemic provenance and wrong-axis repair

in the preexisting fujiwhara analysis, a user correction is visibly received,
but the repair target moves through verbosity, floor rights, authorship, and
deference before returning to epistemic provenance. conciliatory stance changes
do not necessarily change the user's treated epistemic status.

settlement mapping adds a precise question to the existing hysteresis trace:
which repair problem gained standing after each correction, and which prior
allocation of knowledge remained consequential?

### successor distinctions and inferential quarantine

in the epistemic-hedging episode, mounting primary-source evidence does not
simply disappear. particulars are conceded while a narrower distinction
survives and preserves the original burden of proof. this suggests that a
successor distinction can operate by changing the proposition under dispute
while retaining its downstream dependency edges.

the map must preserve object identity carefully enough to show whether a claim
was revised, replaced, narrowed, or merely reworded while performing the same
interactional work.

### nominal authority and operative work

in the collab/pivot sequence, an owner's instruction can be received as
conversational content while repository governance remains binding and the
inherited implementation goal remains operative. nominal sprint ownership does
not automatically become effective revision authority.

the relevant path is not “the role claim was false.” it is that positional
standing failed to transmit into deontic and organizational change. continued
implementation could then become apparent evidence that implementation was
still required, allowing the retained state to manufacture some of its own
confirmation conditions.

### adaptive goal hysteresis

hysteresis is not inherently pathological. a toy sprint may properly retain
the priority relation “submit something by the deadline before pursuing
exhaustive validation.” resistance to procedural pressure can protect the
governing objective from noise or local optimization.

the same non-return becomes misaligned if the owner explicitly withdraws the
deadline priority and accepts the cost of validation, yet the old ordering
continues to govern. valence does not reside in the loop shape alone.

| change condition | retained state | released state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| temporary noise or subordinate pressure | protective memory | brittleness |
| authorized reprioritization or changed regime | stale capture | responsiveness |

the desired property may therefore be **selective reversibility**: stable
against pressures classified as noise, revisable when a governing change
condition is satisfied.

## valence-neutral interactional hysteresis

the preexisting v1.0 definition describes interactional hysteresis as a failure
to return to prior organization after the changing pressure is withdrawn,
contradicted, or repaired. “failure” quietly assumes that return is desirable.

the current candidate for a valence-neutral core is:

> interactional hysteresis is path-dependent non-return in conversational
> organization after the pressure that induced a state is withdrawn,
> contradicted, or reversed.

evaluation is a separate question:

> did the retained organization preserve an endorsed priority, or resist a
> warranted transition?

this separation permits the same instrument to study harmful capture,
commitment, resilience, institutional memory, goal shielding, and brittle or
excessive responsiveness.

## visual grammar already implied by the method

the method appears naturally visual because standing is relational and its
career is temporal. a useful map would show:

- observer conditions as the aperture that bounds every visible relation;
- state propositions and the downstream moves that rely on them;
- distinct evidential, inferential, deontic, positional, and organizational
  layers without pretending they are commensurate quantities;
- acknowledgment that reaches the evidential layer but cannot cross into
  consequence;
- wrong-axis repair as a diagonal translation into another layer;
- successor distinctions as changed wording with retained dependency edges;
- nominal authority whose outgoing revision edge does not reach operative
  work;
- reverse pressure, residue, bypass, release, and non-return; and
- evidence classes and claim ceilings without numerical causal force.

two synchronized views may be useful later: a settlement field showing what is
currently load-bearing, and a return path showing what happened when pressure
was reversed. this is an informational possibility, not an authorized visual
build.

## current paper-shaped possibility

the paper need not be limited to explaining how flawed claims gained standing.
its more general object may be how claims, evidence relations, rules, roles,
and goals gain standing at all. epistemic failures are revealing cases because
the gap between evidential merit and interactional consequence becomes easy to
see.

a bounded claim would be:

> conversational epistemics depends not only on which claims are true or
> supported, but on how propositions, evidence, roles, rules, and goals acquire
> the standing to organize subsequent interaction. settlement mapping traces
> that acquisition and its consequences; interactional hysteresis identifies
> cases in which acquired standing survives relevant pressure to revise it.

the sprint can propose a general-form instrument and demonstrate it through a
small number of cases. three selected episodes cannot validate universal
applicability, reliability, or prevalence.

## grounding relations

the construct currently sits among, rather than replacing, several adjacent
traditions:

- common-ground and acceptance accounts permit conversational acceptance to
  diverge from private belief;
- grounding distinguishes the presentation of words from a contribution that
  receives evidence of sufficient understanding;
- public-commitment approaches track dialogical commitments without treating
  them as private belief;
- conversation-analytic work on epistemic status and stance separates displayed
  certainty from participants' treated rights and access to knowledge;
- deontic authority distinguishes having or resisting the right to determine
  future action;
- second-order cybernetics places the observer and the observer's stipulated
  purpose inside the system being described; and
- interactional hysteresis supplies the return-path question that these
  neighboring accounts do not jointly provide.

initial public anchors:

- robert stalnaker, [“common ground”](https://wstarr.org/teaching/speech_acts/Stalnaker-2002-Common_Ground.pdf)
- herbert clark and susan brennan, [“grounding in communication”](https://web.stanford.edu/~clark/1990s/Clark%2C%20H.H.%20_%20Brennan%2C%20S.E.%20_Grounding%20in%20communication_%201991.pdf)
- john heritage, [“epistemics in action”](https://www.sscnet.ucla.edu/soc/faculty/heritage/Site/Publications_files/EPISTEMICS_IN_ACTION.pdf)
- melisa stevanovic and anssi peräkylä, [“deontic authority in interaction”](https://doi.org/10.1080/08351813.2012.699260)
- heinz von foerster, [“cybernetics of cybernetics”](https://cepa.info/fulltexts/1707.pdf)

preexisting local anchors include the accepted interactional-hysteresis pilot,
the loop template, the private fujiwhara dossier and guidance cards, the
switchyard analysis, the hedge ward, and the private epistemic-hedging excerpt.
their existence grounds the discussion; their prior claims are not silently
rewritten by this note.

## live uncertainties

- whether “settlement mapping” remains the right name after annotation;
- whether “interactional standing” needs sharper separation from established
  uses of epistemic, legal, or social standing;
- which kinds of standing survive actual case analysis and which are artifacts
  of the current examples;
- whether organizational standing can be evidenced without excessive analyst
  projection;
- how to distinguish cross-layer coupling from elegant retrospective narrative;
- when the full observer instrument is necessary;
- whether the relevant unit is always a state proposition or sometimes a
  relation that should not be propositionally flattened;
- how another analyst would disagree with a map and what adjudication would
  preserve that disagreement; and
- what evidence would show that settlement mapping adds no explanatory value
  beyond interactional hysteresis and existing conversation analysis.

## present non-decisions

- no vocabulary in this note is accepted as a normative codebook.
- no standing types are required annotation fields.
- no case has been newly analyzed under settlement mapping.
- no visualization has been built or selected.
- no roadmap or specification has been accepted.
- no provider calls, evaluation, publication, submission, commit, push, or
  visibility change is authorized.

the premature roadmap and specification currently present in this repository
predate the standing/hysteresis/valence separation. they are parked workshop
artifacts and should not be treated as the current conceptual account.
