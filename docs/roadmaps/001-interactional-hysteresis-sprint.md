# where the intervention has to land

## owner view

- programme: develop interactional hysteresis v1.1 through a small
  naturalistic study of how conversational states become load-bearing, resist
  reversal, and respond differently to repair, bypass, or exit.
- why a roadmap: the observer problem, method revision, case analysis,
  comparison, report, and optional evaluation are distinct research slices.
  collapsing them into one spec would make method acceptance silently
  authorize empirical claims and publication work.
- current edge: draft the observer-conditioned settlement-mapping slice. no
  private case analysis, provider call, report claim, publication, submission,
  commit, or push is authorized by this roadmap.

## established ground

- interactional hysteresis v1.0, its template, and prior applications predate
  this sprint.
- three private naturalistic episodes have provisional analytical roles: the
  collab/pivot sequence, the early epistemic-hedging cycle, and the
  desktop-client contrast.
- raw conversations, screenshots, and exports remain private; public material
  is derived, paraphrased, synthetic, or separately cleared.
- the retained python ledger is a historical, unadopted prototype rather than
  the method of this study.
- the current study concerns observable interactional organization. it does
  not claim direct access to model interiors or hidden orchestration.

## provisional capability review

these marks record owner review orientation, not accepted decisions:

## programme invariants

1. the observer does not stand outside the instrument: every analytical claim
   is bounded by a declared slice, projection, attribution policy, and known
   occlusion.
2. surface speaker, causal author, system resolver, and analyst reconstruction
   are not silently collapsed.
3. source availability, source uptake, source-governed behavior, and analyst
   inference remain distinct.
4. method fields do not become empirical findings merely because a template
   contains them.
5. contemporaneous benefit and retrospectively recognized interactional cost
   may coexist.
6. a local intervention can be useful without repairing the mechanism that
   regenerated the state.
7. each slice requires its own owner transition; roadmap order does not confer
   implementation authority.

## slice 00 — observer-conditioned settlement mapping

**status:** now / draft spec under owner review

**outcome:** a small, usable secondary pass for interactional-hysteresis traces
that records what a declared projection treats as established, binding,
authorized, or operative without claiming truth throughout the machine.

**work:**

- specify the minimum observer declaration;
- distinguish apparent speaker attribution from causal authorship;
- define settlement events, persistence, release, and cross-status coupling;
- decide what remains optional or extensible from the larger
  `t, pi, rho, alpha, mu` observer instrument; and
- calibrate the draft on one bounded trace and one boundary case.

**dependencies:** the existing v1.0 method, the owner discussion recorded in
this roadmap, and access to one owner-selected private calibration passage.

**exit:** the owner can review one method note, one field template, and two
derived traces and decide whether the construct should be retained, renamed,
revised, or abandoned.

**guard:** do not reconstruct hidden multi-agent machinery, treat the four
settlement statuses as ontological truth types, or begin the three result-case
analysis under this slice.

## slice 01 — method calibration and case protocol

**status:** later / blocked on slice 00 disposition

**outcome:** an interactional-hysteresis v1.1 case protocol whose fields have
survived calibration and whose evidential ceilings are explicit.

**work:**

- reconcile settlement mapping with baseline, pressure, displacement, pawl,
  ratchet, reverse pressure, release, residue, and classification;
- test successor distinction, salvage effect, terminal disposition, and the
  intervention delivery audit against preexisting applications;
- define the unit of analysis, source-locator practice, alternative
  explanations, and defeat conditions; and
- separate required fields from optional diagnostics.

**dependencies:** owner disposition of slice 00 and the preexisting fujiwhara,
switchyard, template, and guidance materials.

**exit:** a versioned method and case-matrix contract can be applied by another
careful analyst without converting uncertainty into a required finding.

**guard:** do not tune the method until every selected result case exhibits the
preferred mechanism.

## slice 02 — three bounded naturalistic analyses

**status:** later / blocked on the case protocol

**outcome:** three private, source-located episode analyses with separate
observations, inferences, owner retrospection, alternatives, and causal
ceilings.

**work:**

- analyze the collab/pivot sequence as the main settlement-coupling episode;
- analyze the epistemic-hedging cycle as the clearest candidate successor
  distinction;
- analyze the desktop-client contrast as a causal-altitude boundary case; and
- produce public-safe derived traces without copying private wording.

**dependencies:** an accepted slice-01 protocol and a separately confirmed
private-analysis boundary.

**exit:** every candidate finding has an opaque source locator, evidence
status, retained alternative, maximum classification, and terminal
disposition.

**guard:** do not turn selected cases into prevalence evidence or use later
owner understanding to overwrite contemporaneous mixed benefit.

## slice 03 — comparative mechanism and intervention map

**status:** later / blocked on three complete cases

**outcome:** a cross-case account of where an intervention must land and what a
failed intervention can discriminate.

**work:**

- compare pawls, ratchets, residue, release, and cross-status coupling;
- distinguish utterance-level repair from context, client-substrate, and
  participation-level intervention;
- audit assignment, receipt, uptake, persistence, and regeneration; and
- state predicted downstream differences and defeat conditions.

**dependencies:** slice 02 evidence, not merely the method's available labels.

**exit:** a comparison table and synthetic event trace support a bounded
partial-mechanism claim while preserving live alternatives.

**guard:** do not credit compensatory salvage as proof that the original
friction was necessary or well targeted.

## slice 04 — report construction and publication review

**status:** later / blocked on comparative result

**outcome:** a concise sprint report that makes the preexisting/sprint-native
boundary, evidential ceiling, privacy boundary, and dual-use implications
legible.

**work:**

- draft the report in the official structure;
- verify claims and references;
- render and inspect the pdf;
- review every publication-sensitive paraphrase; and
- preserve submission as a separate owner action.

**dependencies:** owner-reviewed outputs from slices 01 through 03.

**exit:** a local final candidate satisfies the sprint checklist and has no
unreviewed private-source projection.

**guard:** report completion does not authorize publication, submission,
commit, push, or visibility change.

## slice 05 — optional matched-vignette evaluation

**status:** optional / separately authorized

**outcome:** if still useful, a small evaluation tests a mechanism-matched
intervention against a defined baseline without becoming a prerequisite for
the observational report.

**work:**

- specify vignettes, scoring, sampling, model and client conditions, cost,
  privacy, and failure interpretation;
- distinguish intervention assignment from actual delivery and uptake; and
- preserve all prompts, outputs, and exclusions for audit.

**dependencies:** report-complete observational claims and explicit provider
authorization.

**exit:** either an authorized evaluation produces auditable results or the
report names it honestly as future work.

**guard:** no provider calls, credential use, or live-system operation follow
from this roadmap.

## programme proof

- source-linked method artifacts and private case matrices;
- public-safe derived traces checked against their private sources;
- explicit claim-to-evidence and alternative-explanation tables;
- deterministic documentation and link checks;
- rendered report inspection; and
- owner transitions recorded without inferring acceptance from enthusiasm,
  review marks, or artifact traversal.

## unresolved currents

- whether “settlement mapping” survives calibration as the right term;
- whether the minimum observer declaration needs an explicit reference resolver
  or operation modality in this study;
- whether settlement statuses are best represented as four named predicates,
  looser questions, or a different structure generated by the cases;
- which bounded passage should serve as the first calibration trace; and
- whether the sprint window leaves any honest room for optional evaluation.
